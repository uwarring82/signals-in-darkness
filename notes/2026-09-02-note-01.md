## Findings against the card

### 1. Extremum channel (card §Extremum information) — confirmed
Exact D_Bern vs leading order: within 6 % for s²C̄/(1−C̄²) ≤ 0.06, within 15–20 % at 0.12–0.17, factor 1.7 at 0.5, factor 3–10 beyond 1. The validity condition in the card is the right parameter; "≪" means ≲ 0.1 for 10 % accuracy.

### 2. Mid-fringe channel (card §Mid-fringe information) — leading order is wrong where it matters
| C̄ | s | τ_c/c | card LO | ½Σr_k² (exact r_k) | GP comparator | exact HMM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.4 | 0.5 | 1 | 1.25e-4 | 7.6e-5 | 7.5e-5 | 7.5e-5 |
| 0.4 | 0.5 | 5 | 1.63e-3 | 9.9e-4 | 8.9e-4 | 8.9e-4 |
| 0.4 | 0.5 | 20 | 7.6e-3 | 4.7e-3 | 3.1e-3 | 3.1e-3 |
| 0.4 | 0.5 | 60 | 2.4e-2 | 1.4e-2 | 5.9e-3 | 5.9e-3 |
| 0.9 | 0.3 | 5 | 5.4e-3 | 4.5e-3 | 3.6e-3 | 3.9e-3 |
| 0.4 | 1.0 | 20 | 1.2e-1 | 1.9e-2 | 1.0e-2 | 1.0e-2 |

Two separate corrections:

(a) The lag correlation is r_k = ½C̄²[e^{−s²(1−a_k)} − e^{−s²(1+a_k)}] ≈ C̄²s²a_k·e^{−s²}, not C̄²s²a_k. The factor e^{−2s²} on the rate (0.61 at s = 0.5) is the same contrast-loss physics as at the extremum: modulation also reduces the effective slope. Cheap to include; should be.

(b) The ½Σr_k² expansion fails once the *spectral* perturbation at zero frequency, δ(0) = 2Σ_k r_k ≈ 2C̄²s²τ_c/c, is of order one — a condition first order in s² and linear in τ_c/c. The card's boxed condition C̄⁴s⁴Σa_k² ≪ 1 is the wrong parameter: it is 0.015 in the τ_c/c = 20 row where the expansion is already off by 2.5×. (My own earlier statement of the condition as Σr_k² ≪ 1 was equally wrong.) Physically: at long τ_c the mid-fringe channel stops being fourth-order variance detection and becomes tracking of a quasi-static offset, whose rate is first order in s² and saturates toward ~C̄²s²/2. The transition is soft (logarithmic), so the LO formula overstates the mid-fringe rate by ×2 at τ_c/c ≈ 15 and ×4 at 60 for s = 0.5, C̄ = 0.4.

(c) The Gaussian-process comparator with exact r_k reproduces the exact binary HMM rate to within 1–5 % in every case tested (7 % at C̄ = 0.9). With geometric r_k the comparator has a closed form: for S(ω) = [α+β cos ω]/[1−2a cos ω+a²], α = 1+a²−2ρa², β = 2a(ρ−1), the rate is −½ log[α/(1+b²)] with b = (−α+√(α²−β²))/β. This replaces both the LO formula and the HMM for the regime map. The card's remark "not exact for binary observations" is true but immaterial at this accuracy; the HMM stays as validation only.

HMM check at C̄ = 0.4, s = 0.5: crossover 6.3 (interpolated on a coarse grid; GP says 7.2). The leading-order threshold is accurate only for s ≲ 0.2; at the experimentally useful s ≈ 0.5 it is 50 % low, and at high contrast the extremum's exact divergence grows so fast that mid-fringe never catches up. The map must be drawn at a stated s (or as a family), not as an amplitude-free figure.

### 4. Endpoint lemma (card §Endpoint-optimality) — holds within resolution; profile is flat near mid-fringe
C̄ = 0.9, s = 0.3, τ_c/c = 5 (near the crossover), exact HMM, N = 2.5e5 × 3 seeds:
θ = 0°: 3.90(18)e-3; 20°: 3.00(7)e-3; 40°: 3.03(11)e-3; 60°: 3.62(11)e-3; 75°: 3.67(14)e-3; 85°: 3.64(16)e-3; 90°: 3.67(16)e-3.
Interior minimum confirmed; no interior point beats the endpoints; 60–90° is flat to within errors. Two cases away from the crossover (C̄ = 0.4, s = 0.5, τ_c/c = 20 and 1) give clean monotone profiles with the predicted endpoint winning. Note the extremum HMM value (3.90e-3) sits ~6 % above the marginal D_Bern (3.69e-3): at high contrast the extremum bit stream carries a little correlation information of its own. The lemma's leading-order form is a lower bound there.

### 5. Filtered-OU corollary (card §Hidden process) — confirmed
Closed forms s_τ² = 2g²σ_x²[τ_cτ − τ_c²(1−e^{−τ/τ_c})] and a_k = e^{−kc/τ_c}·(cosh u − 1)/(u − 1 + e^{−u}), u = τ/τ_c, match numerical double integrals to all printed digits (four cases). Consequence: for τ ≫ τ_c, a_1 ≈ (τ_c/2τ)e^{−(c−τ)/τ_c} — long interrogations wash out shot-to-shot correlation.

### 6. Regime map (card §Principal figure)
(i) Point-sampled, same τ, leading order (`sid_regime_map.png`): the crossover in shot units is U-shaped in C̄ with minimum 2.9 shots at C̄ = 1/√2 and 4.65 shots at realistic parity contrast 0.4 — low contrast also favours the extremum because the correlation channel needs two good bits. Analytic: τ_c/c = 2/ln[1 + 4C̄²(1−C̄²)].
(ii) After τ optimisation with exponential contrast decay, T₂ = 10 t_dead, gσ_xT₂ = 0.5 rad, exact extremum divergence and GP mid-fringe rate (`sid_regime_map_tau_optimised_exact.png`): mid-fringe wins only for τ_c/t_dead ≳ 210 (C̄₀ = 0.4), 140 (0.5), 75 (0.7), 45 (0.9). The extremum wins everywhere else by lengthening τ to ≈ T₂ and integrating the noise (s_τ⁴ ∝ τ⁴ for τ ≪ τ_c); the mid-fringe winner keeps τ ≈ 0.3 T₂ to protect correlations and C̄⁴. In shot units the crossover is a handful of shots; in physical units, once τ is a free action, it is several T₂.

### 7. Oracle CUSUM delay (card §Adaptive policy, oracle half) — rate predicts delay to ≈10 %
γ = 10³, h = 6.9, C̄ = 0.4, s = 0.5:
τ_c/c = 20: extremum 4340(250) vs h/I = 5330; mid-fringe 2010(170) vs h/I_exact = 2240 (card LO would give 910).
τ_c/c = 5: extremum 4260(240); mid-fringe 7130(660) vs h/I_exact = 7780 — extremum wins by 1.7× where the card's LO put the crossover.
τ_c/c = 1: mid-fringe 79 000(6800) vs 92 700.
Delays run 0.8–0.9 of h/I because the CUSUM reflection shortens first passage when the increment variance is large relative to the drift; correlation does not hurt. The exact rate is a usable delay predictor; the card's mid-fringe LO rate is not.

## Scouting comparison
- Item 2 of the earlier scouting (validity condition Σr_k² ≪ 1) was wrong in the same direction as the card: the operative parameter is δ(0) = 2C̄²s²Σa_k, first order in s². Corrected here.
- Item 6 (amplitude independence): true at leading order only. The exact crossover moves by 50 % between s → 0 and s = 0.5 and disappears at C̄ = 0.99. The card cannot promise an amplitude-free map.
- Item 7 (tractable detector loses a constant): moot for the single-latent case — the grid HMM is the exact detector and is cheap (10⁵ shots/s). It returns with two latent processes (servo regime).
- The structural point "extremum = dephasing measurement integrating over τ" appears quantitatively in map (ii): allowing τ as an action moves the crossover from a few shots to several T₂.
- New, not scouted: the mid-fringe channel changes order in s at long τ_c (variance detection → tracking), which is what breaks the LO expansion; and the Gaussian-process closed form is a near-exact replacement for the HMM rate.
