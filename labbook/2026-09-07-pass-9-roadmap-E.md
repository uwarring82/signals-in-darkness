# 2026-09-07 — Pass 9: the regression gate caught me before it caught the archive

**Question:** Restate identifiability dimensionlessly in T₂, add the second principal-regime contrast, and require the 24 historical rows to reproduce `res7A` within 6 × 10⁻⁸.
**Finding:** The physics is unchanged under the reparameterisation — s²(u,v) = G²·2(vu − v²(1−e^{−u/v})) is algebraically identical to the old form — so the shared rows should have reproduced to machine precision. They did not. The first run sat 6.15 × 10⁻⁷ *above* `res7A`, with three optimiser starts landing on three points spread over 1.1 %. The cause was the reparameterisation itself: expressing the class-B infimum over Γ/Γ̂ instead of Γ rescales the variable by T₂, and L-BFGS-B's default `ftol` divides by max(|f|,1), so on an objective of order 10⁻⁶ it stops on a relative change of ~3 × 10⁻⁴. Both parameterisations were under-converged; the old one merely stopped lower. Tightened, all three starts converge to 6.002659924203 × 10⁻⁶ and **`res7A`'s stored value is 3.8 × 10⁻¹⁶ above that** — the archive was right and my run was wrong. A 401 × 401 grid confirms it.
**Why it matters:** The gate did the job a gate exists for, and it did it in the direction that is easy to miss — catching the new work rather than the old. Over-correcting then produced its own defect: at `ftol=1e-18, gtol=1e-14`, 46 of 108 starts returned `ABNORMAL_TERMINATION_IN_LNSRCH` while still reporting the right minimum, because scipy differentiates this objective by finite differences and the requested `gtol` sat below the gradient's noise. Shipping that would have made the recorded convergence status meaningless in 43 % of cases. The setting shipped converges all 108 starts with an across-start spread of 2.4 × 10⁻⁹, twenty-five times inside the tolerance. This also revises what the archive's 6 × 10⁻⁸ *means*: it is default-tolerance under-convergence, not an intrinsic optimiser-dependence.
