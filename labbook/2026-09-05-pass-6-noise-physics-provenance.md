# 2026-09-05 — Pass 6: whose criterion is the validity map?

**Question:** Does the prior art behind C23 reach the layer at which C23 actually operates — evaluating an expansion's own error criterion *on the crossover curve* — or does it stop at the noise physics beneath it?
**Finding:** It reaches it, as a method. Hohenberg and Krekhov (Phys. Rep. 572, Sec. V.B, pp. 17–18, read at full text from the PDF) test the self-consistency of mean-field theory using the Ginzburg–Landau expansion's own fluctuation estimate, obtain a parameter-dependent validity region, and contrast conventional superconductors against high-T_c materials — the same logical shape as a boundary that is valid over part of the (C̄, s) plane and not the rest. So the record now reads: **C23 is a Ginzburg-style self-consistency test applied to a binary-information crossover.** It does *not* read that C23 is the Levanyuk–Ginzburg criterion; that name belongs to fluctuation corrections approaching a single critical point, whereas C23 tests pointwise along a curve. Hohenberg–Krekhov is cited directly; Levanyuk and Ginzburg stay marked second-hand until their originals are read. The sensing-specific instantiation was not found — but the Layer-3 search was title-and-abstract only, and this is exactly the move that lives in a footnote, so "not found" is the honest phrasing and "does not exist" is not available.
**Why it matters:** Two claims are re-scoped in attribution and neither changes status, because ledger status records evidential standing, not originality. C02's correlator is conceded outright: it is Wudarski et al. Eq. (29) at φ_R = π/2, verified numerically to 3.1 × 10⁻¹⁵ over an 18-point grid, with Eq. (34) giving a_k. What remains the project's is the correlator's *use* inside the spectral information-rate comparator and the sequential crossover. Two sources were also corrected against themselves: Klauder & Anderson is citable for echo structure only (their Gaussian diffusion is non-stationary), and Degen's Eq. (20) is the unit-contrast case, so the finite-contrast extremum divergence is not in that review — though the review does name and separately analyse both operating points, which the project must concede.
**Status:** unchanged (C23, C21, C03); re-scoped in attribution only (C02, status `result`); scope narrowed in wording (C04). Roadmap A closed.
