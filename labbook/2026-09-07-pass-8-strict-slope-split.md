**Question:** Is the mid-fringe leading-order helper wrong, and what does B's second operating point have to be?
**Finding:** The helper was not wrong; its name was. `I_strict = ½C̄⁴s⁴Σa_k²` and `I_slope = I_strict·e^{−2s²}` are both legitimate — they agree through strict O(s⁴) and diverge above it, by 19.7 % at s = 0.3 and 64.9 % at s = 0.5 — and the former `I_mid_lo` computed the first under a name, and one figure legend, promising the second.
