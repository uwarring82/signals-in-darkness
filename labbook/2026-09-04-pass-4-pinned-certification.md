# 2026-09-04 — Pass 4: the whole path under the environment it claims

**Question:** Does the analysis reproduce under the environment `environment.yml` pins, rather than whatever happened to be installed — and does the repaired route's gate actually reject?
**Finding:** Yes on both counts. Under python 3.12.14 / numpy 2.4.4 / scipy 1.17.1 the archive reproduces within declared tolerances, the largest deviation being 1.7 × 10⁻¹² against a 10⁻⁹ criterion. Five of seven calibration rows and eleven of fifteen delay rows reproduce **bit-identically**, and the four that move are exactly the four measured at the two thresholds withdrawn in pass 3 — the same rejection, on the same field, under a different interpreter and a different numpy major version. No version sensitivity was detected across the tested Python and NumPy versions on this execution path. Two producers had never run to completion: `sid_run2.py` died at a `\tfrac` mathtext macro that no matplotlib version defines, and because its `json.dump` sat after the figure, every run destroyed its own output — which is why the archived `res2_partial.json` was a hand reconstruction missing key F2 entirely.
**Why it matters:** The withdrawal of the two calibration rows is now a fact about those rows rather than about the environment that found it. Running the path also exposed that the principal regime figure drew two validity criteria the ledger had already withdrawn, placing the mid-fringe boundary at τ_c/c = 376 where the card's own criterion puts it at 5.29 — just above the crossover the same figure draws. Under the correct criterion the leading-order crossover lies inside its own validity region only for C̄ ≈ 0.2–0.5.
**Status:** result (C19, C20, C21, C23, C24 as then recorded); withdrawn (C22, the superseded overlays).
