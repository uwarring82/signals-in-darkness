# 2026-09-11 — Pass 19: the class the ladder ranges over did not exist yet

**Question:** Can roadmap C's L1/L2/L3 rungs be computed at all, given that the benchmark ladder ranges over thirteen candidates?
**Finding:** No, and the gap was larger than a missing measurement. Seven of the eleven frozen schedules had never been calibrated, and **no candidate had ever been run on the selection stream**, including the six inherited from roadmap B. The cheap repair — redefine L3 over whatever had historical outputs — was rejected as replacing performance-driven selection with availability-driven selection, which is exactly what freezing the class before unblinding was meant to prevent. The honest alternative to the full campaign was to stop at L2 and leave L3 open, not to redefine L3 after seeing its cost.
**Why it matters:** Roadmap B measured the class through two separate hard-coded lists — `build_policies` for the constants and interleaves, `sid_run8.B_VALUES` for the age-based switches — so extending the class meant editing both, which is how an executed class drifts from the manifest that froze it. Everything now comes from `analysis/schedule_class_C.json` through one factory, and `run_batch` absorbed the age tracking that `run_batch_age` used to own. Replacing a measured implementation is only safe if the replacement reproduces it, and means are not enough because the ladder reuses per-replicate outputs: the equivalence is asserted **per replicate**, at both operating points, on both streams, and it holds bit-for-bit in all three comparisons.
**Status:** Full thirteen-candidate campaign authorised and begun. Phase made mandatory everywhere; the operating point is no longer defaulted either.
**Next test:** calibrate all twenty-two cells and freeze eligibility before any selection delay exists.
